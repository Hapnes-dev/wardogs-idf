# Privacy policy — WARDOGS IDF

Last updated: 11 September 2026

## The short version

WARDOGS IDF collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing.

## What the app collects

None. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting SDK and no third-party libraries that phone home. It asks for no
Android runtime permissions.

## What the app sends

Nothing. The app declares no `INTERNET` permission, so Android will not let it
open a network connection even if a future bug tried to. It works with the
device in flight mode.

## What the app stores

The coordinates you type live in memory while the screen is open and survive a
screen rotation. They are discarded when the app is closed. Nothing is written
to disk, to a cloud service or to another app.

## Children

The app is a utility for a video game and is not directed at children under 13.
Because it collects no data at all, no personal information of any user, of any
age, can be gathered by it.

## Changes

If a future version ever collects or transmits anything, this policy will be
updated before that version is published, and the change will be described here
rather than silently applied.
